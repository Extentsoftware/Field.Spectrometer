## Request format for quotations

Record part ID, manufacturer/order code, specification/drawing, quantity, unit price, currency, tax basis, shipping destination/cost, lead time, quote date/expiry and warranty. Put the resulting selection and purchasing status in the BOM. Retain private correspondence separately; only publish redacted quotation details.
