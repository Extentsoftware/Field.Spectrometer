## Costing method

Track unit price, buy quantity, shipping, taxes, assembly and consumables separately; record date, exact supplier and currency. Distinguish prototype development costs from one-unit build cost. The historical HYD1 quote alone is not a total project or delivered price. An ROI estimate requires measured operating costs, adoption and independently validated performance.
